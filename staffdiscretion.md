## Staff Discretion & Rule Enforcement

BandidosRP is not governed by rigid technicalities. Our rules exist to support **collaborative storytelling**, not to be gamed, skirted, or loopholed. Staff will always prioritize the **intent** of your actions over the exact wording of any rule.

---

### 🧠 Rules Are a Framework, Not a Trap

- Our goal is to **guide roleplay**, not punish players.
- If your behavior goes against the **spirit** of the rules—even if it technically doesn’t “break” them—you may still be subject to staff action.
- When in doubt, ask yourself:
  - “Would this break immersion?”
  - “Is this helping or harming the story?”
  - “Would other players feel disrespected by this?”

---

### 🔄 Staff Procedures & Adjustments

- BandidosRP staff may **adjust how rules are enforced** or interpreted depending on context.
- These changes will be:
  - Based on **what’s best for the community**
  - Communicated clearly through **Discord announcements** or **ticket responses**
  - Documented internally for consistency

---

### 💬 Open Communication

- Players are welcome to ask for clarification on rules.
- Staff may reach out **privately or via Discord tickets** to discuss an issue before taking action.
- If something feels unfair or unclear, **use the ticket system** to open a respectful conversation.

---

### ❗ Accountability

- Harassment, abuse of systems, or bad-faith play will not be tolerated—regardless of “rules lawyering.”
- The server’s health, story balance, and the respect between players always take priority.
- Warnings, suspensions, or bans may be issued when:
  - A player repeatedly ignores staff feedback
  - Conflict or behavior becomes toxic
  - Rule circumvention disrupts the experience for others

---

### 🧵 Player Responsibility

- You are expected to **know the rules** and **seek clarity** when needed.
- “I didn’t know” is not always a defense, especially for repeat offenses.
- We ask every player to treat staff and fellow players with the same care and professionalism that they expect in return.

---

Staff are not here to control the story—  
We’re here to **protect it**, **guide it**, and help it become something worth remembering.
