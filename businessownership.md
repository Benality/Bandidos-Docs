## Business Ownership & Joblocked Roles

In BandidosRP, businesses are **claimed and operated through roleplay**, not through systems or permissions. If you want to run a shop, saloon, or trading post — all you need to do is show up and roleplay it.

---

### 🔓 Open Business Ownership

- There are **no whitelisted businesses** or job-locked stores.
- If you want to run the saloon, then **station your character there**, serve customers, and build a reputation.
- Your ownership is based on your **activity, story presence, and consistency**, not a flag in a database.

> Business ownership is recognized through roleplay and reputation, not scripts.

---

### 🏢 Joblocked Roles

While most of the world is open, certain **civil service roles** are locked to ensure consistency, realism, and quality of experience for others.

#### 🚨 The following roles are joblocked:

- **Sheriff & Deputies**
- **Doctors & Medical Staff**

#### 🏛️ Additional joblocked roles may include:

- **Rangers**
- **Train Conductors**
- **Government Officials**

These roles are **limited-access** due to their impact on the world, and will be assigned or allowlisted by staff.

---

### 🏨 Business Ideas & Locations

You can run a business from:

- Town storefronts
- Remote lodges or camps
- Motels and rentals
- Farms, ranches, or cabins

Businesses may be:

- **Legal** (e.g. saloon, clinic, blacksmith)
- **Illegal** (e.g. speakeasy, underground chemist, gunrunner)

Just make it immersive, consistent, and rooted in roleplay.

---

### 🏷️ Custom Item Branding

Use the `item_labels` system to give any item a custom name and description.

- Works on crafted or purchased items (food, drink, medicine, etc.)
- Great for branding your business products (e.g. “Carmela’s Fire Brew”)
- Item effects **do not change** — only the flavor text does

> This is a powerful RP tool to make your shop unique.

#### ❌ Prohibited Item Relabeling:

You **may not** relabel items that:

- Contain metadata (e.g. amount of money, durability, etc.)
- Are considered sensitive to economy or combat balance

Examples of restricted items:

- `money_stack`
- Weapons
- Durability-based tools

---

### 📦 Inventory & Stashes

- Use **stashes** to manage business inventory
- They can be placed in your property or storefront
- **Protect them** — if you're online, they can be broken into

Remember: stash raids must be **story-driven**, not random or spammed.

---

Owning a business in BandidosRP means building something people care about.  
No forms, no flags — just your story, your effort, and your presence.
