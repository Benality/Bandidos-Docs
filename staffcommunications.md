# Staff Communication & Expected Conduct

## 🎟️ Contacting Staff

* All official staff communication takes place through the **BandidosRP Discord Support Bot**.
* For general issues or questions, open a support ticket.
* Player reports are to be sent to the same Discord bot. Please do not submit player reports in any public channel.

## 🧾 Confidentiality & Respect

* Communication between players and staff is considered **confidential**. Staff will not publicly share the details of reports or conversations unless the reporting player breaks confidentiality first.
* Both **staff and community members** are expected to act with **respect, maturity, and professionalism** at all times.
* Harassment, passive-aggressive behavior, or public shaming of staff or players will not be tolerated and may result in disciplinary action.

## 🗨️ Staff Feedback

* Constructive feedback toward staff, decisions, or systems is welcome—but must be **respectfully communicated** through appropriate channels (e.g., feedback forms, designated Discord threads).
* Witch hunting, rumor spreading, or rallying others against a staff decision will result in immediate removal from the community.

## ⚖️ Staff Discretion

* BandidosRP Staff may exercise **discretion** in interpreting and enforcing rules based on **intent, context, and impact**.
* Not every situation fits perfectly into a rule—staff will work with players in good faith to guide better outcomes.
* **Repeat offenders or low-effort roleplayers** may receive additional scrutiny even for minor offenses, as part of maintaining overall community quality.

## 🚫 Staff Harassment Policy

* If you are unhappy with a staff decision or feel mistreated, do **not take it out on individual staff members**. Instead, escalate via the proper feedback or appeal channels.
* Harassment of staff in DMs, in-game, or in public Discord spaces may result in **removal from the community without warning**.
