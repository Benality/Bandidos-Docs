## Serial Killers, Villainy & Special Characters

BandidosRP is a collaborative storytelling server. Extreme characters—those who commit serial killings, public executions, or psychological manipulation—can be impactful, but they must be done with **staff knowledge**, **player respect**, and **narrative purpose**.

---

### 🔪 Serial Killers & High-Violence Characters

- Characters whose primary arc involves **repeated or ritualistic killing** must be:
  - **Approved by staff before execution of the concept**
  - Monitored to ensure they don’t disrupt the larger community
- These characters must:
  - Prioritize **storytelling over body count**
  - Use fear, clues, and buildup—not shock value or “slasher” gameplay
  - **Avoid random or excessive violence** just to stir activity

> **Reminder:** Killing for kill’s sake is not RP. Every death must matter to someone, especially when done in secret or ritual.

---

### 🎭 Antagonists & “Villain” RP

- You don’t need permission to play a **villainous character**, but the line is drawn at:

  - Characters who torture without consent
  - Repeated kidnapping/hostage use
  - Abusive or manipulative behavior toward players OOC
  - Mass-scale destruction or server disruption

- Strong antagonists should:
  - Have **flaws, motivations, and nuance**
  - **Push others to react**, not control their reactions
  - Be ready for consequences, including law pursuit or character downfall

> Villains who run without facing consequences may have their arcs forcefully closed by staff.

---

### 🧠 Staff-Controlled or Special Characters

Some characters may be **temporarily approved** by staff for:

- Events (e.g. bounty hunters, lawless raiders)
- Lore reveals (e.g. a dying witness with key info)
- Narrative pivots (e.g. cults, outlaws, disappearances)

These characters are typically:

- Time-limited or built to die
- Played by staff or trusted volunteers
- **Not allowed to hoard loot, create factions, or derail player stories**

---

Villains make heroes better.  
But in BandidosRP, even darkness must be deliberate—and leave behind more than just blood.
