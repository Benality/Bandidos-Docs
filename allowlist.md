## Allowlist & Transient Player Policy

BandidosRP allows anyone to join the server freely to explore the world, meet others, and try out the experience before fully committing. These players are known as **Transient Players**.

---

### 🚶 Who Are Transient Players?

Transient players are **not allowlisted** and have joined the server without completing the full application process. This open access is intentional—designed to let you get a feel for the world, community, and roleplay style.

---

### 🔒 Transient Player Limitations

While in this status, the following restrictions apply:

- ❌ **PVP is disabled**
- ❌ **Cannot rob other players**
- ❌ **Cannot be robbed** by other players
- ❌ **Cannot use the bank system**
- ✅ **Can participate in robbery scenes** as hostages or bystanders (but not initiators)
- ✅ **Can use the Job Board**
- ✅ **Can buy a motel room**
- ✅ **Can buy simple weapons**:
  - Bow
  - Cattleman Revolver
  - Bolt Action Rifle  
    (for hunting and self-defense only)

---

### 🌟 Becoming Allowlisted

To unlock full access to the server and become a **Citizen of BandidosRP**, you must complete the allowlist process:

1. **Join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/z7RAT5qq4S)**
2. **Submit your allowlist application**
3. **Complete the interview process with staff**

Once allowlisted, players gain access to:

- Advanced weaponry & shops
- Banking and faction systems
- Full PVP
- Criminal and law RP
- Property upgrades and business ownership
- Participation in long-term storylines

---

### 🕰️ No Time Limit, But…

There is **no deadline** for becoming allowlisted. However, we strongly encourage all players to apply when they’re ready to engage more deeply. Becoming allowlisted unlocks the full BandidosRP experience and allows for **character progression**, **faction participation**, and **meaningful long-term storytelling**.
