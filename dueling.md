## Dueling Policy

Duels are a part of Western tradition and can be a powerful way to settle disputes—when done with purpose. BandidosRP supports duels as **story-driven events**, not casual PvP.

### 🪶 What Counts as a Duel?

A duel is defined as any **one-on-one agreed combat**, usually:

- To settle a personal or ideological conflict
- To resolve tension without escalating into a full group war
- As a challenge to authority, honor, or legacy

Duels may be with:

- Pistols
- Melee weapons
- Bare fists (for informal challenges)

---

### ✅ Duel Rules

- **Both parties must agree, following initiation** before the duel begins.
- A **witness is recommended** for each side, but not required.
- Duels must have **build-up**.
- Once agreed upon, **the duel is final**. The outcome should be respected.

#### 🧠 NVL Still Applies

- The loser of a duel must **value their life** and follow through on consequences—this may include:
  - Surrendering land, items, or titles
  - Leaving town or disbanding a gang
  - Facing death or exile, if previously agreed

#### ⚠️ No Duel Loopholes

- You may not force a duel, or not give the other party any other option but to duel or face negative consquences.
- Dueling to bait someone into a PvP scene without consent will be treated as **meta or powergaming**.

---

### 💀 Duels & Perma

- Perma is never required, even in a duel to the death.
- If both parties **agree in advance** that perma is on the table, it must be clear in OOC beforehand.
- Otherwise, the defeated player decides the outcome:
  - Near-death or long-term injury
  - Capture, exile, or other non-lethal consequence

---

In BandidosRP, a duel is a statement. It should reflect your character’s pride, pain, or desperation—  
not a shortcut to avoid consequences or push PvP.
