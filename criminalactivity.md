## Criminal Activity Rules

Criminal activity in BandidosRP is meant to be **high-stakes**, **story-driven**, and **player-led**. These events should push forward rivalries, build tension with law, and reward well-planned risk—not casual grinding or win-focused play.

---

### 🗝️ Jailbreaks & Escaping from Custody

- Once a wanted character is in **custody or inside a jail cell**, any attempt to break them out counts as a **jailbreak**.
- Jailbreaks must be:
  - **Story-driven** — not repeated for convenience or farming action
  - **Reasonable** — avoid jailbreaking someone you barely know
  - **Well-paced** — don’t immediately jailbreak someone who just got caught

#### 🔁 Cooldowns

- **Gang Members** may jailbreak their own at any time—no cooldowns apply—but staff may step in if it’s abused. Make sure to follow above guidelines.
- **Non-gang characters** must wait **4 days** before initiating another jailbreak.
- You cannot jailbreak with one character, then do it again on another during that cooldown period.

> **Note:** Law enforcement is encouraged to respond seriously to jailbreaks and may escalate their investigations accordingly.

---

### 🏦 Bank Robberies

- A maximum of **6 criminal players** may participate in a single bank robbery.
- Once the robbery starts (e.g., breach initiated), the robbery is considered **in progress**.

#### 📆 Robbery Cooldowns

- Each player may only rob **one bank every 4 days**.
- If you rob a bank on one character, you **cannot** rob another on a different character during that cooldown.

#### 👥 Third-Party Interference

- No third parties may interfere with the robbery—either to assist or oppose.
- Always keep in mind the conflict limits policy.

#### 🏃 Aftermath Expectations

- Getaways should be immersive—not cinematic speedruns.
- If you escape the law, you’re expected to **lay low**. Jumping into another crime immediately may be flagged for **poor pacing**.

---

### 🚂 Train Robberies

- A maximum of **6 criminal players** may rob a train.
- Robbers may **board ahead of time in disguise**, but must clearly announce their intent **once the robbery begins**.

#### 🎭 Role of Law

- Responding law may ask how many outlaws and hostages are present. Robbers AND law, are under no obligation to disclose how many people are in each group. However, it required the groups are aware of and follow the group-size limits.

Criminal scenes should be **tense, risky, and full of story**.  
Every job should have **consequences**, **reactions**, and a **reason** to be remembered.
