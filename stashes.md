## Stash Rules

Stashes are personal storage spaces used for managing inventory, business stock, or hiding items. They are intentionally limited and must be used responsibly to maintain fair and immersive gameplay.

---

### 🔑 Stash Ownership & Limits

- **You may only place one (1) stash per character.**
  - This is a strict limit — attempting to circumvent this, by using an alt to place another stash your main character knows the code will result in administrator actions. Review alt character policy
- **You are responsible for managing, securing, and protecting your stash.**
- **You may share your stash code** with others if you want to give them access.
  - Anyone with the code can open the stash, so be mindful who you trust.

---

### 🧹 Deleting or Resetting Your Stash

- You may **delete your stash** at any time **as long as it is completely empty**.
- If you’ve placed a stash in a location you can no longer access (e.g., terrain bug or property change), you can use the `/resetStash` command:
  - You must be:
    - **Nearby the stash**
    - **Its owner**
    - The **stash must be empty**
  - This will permanently delete the stash so you can place a new one.

---

### 🔓 Break-In Rules

- **Stashes cannot be broken into while the owner is offline.**
- **If the stash owner is online**, their stash is vulnerable and can be broken into by others.
  - Break-ins must be:
    - **Driven by roleplay**
    - **Targeted with IC knowledge**
    - **Not random or loot-hunting behavior**
- **Abuse of stash raids** (e.g. metagaming locations, server-hopping to hit them) will result in fail RP consequences and staff action.

---

### ⚠️ Additional Notes

- Stashes are not safes — they’re **tools for world-building**.
- Their purpose is to:
  - Support business inventory
  - Encourage conflict
  - Enable risk and reward for both criminals and civilians

---

Treat your stash like a secret you’re proud to protect — or daring enough to let others try to find.
