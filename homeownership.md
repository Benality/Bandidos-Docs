## Housing & Property Ownership

In BandidosRP, players can own homes directly through **in-world interaction**—no menus, deeds, or admin approvals are required. Housing is meant to give players a personal stake in the world without heavy mechanics.

---

### 🛒 Buying a Home

- To purchase a home, simply **visit the property in-game** and complete the purchase through the housing interface or assigned system.
- Ownership is tied to your **character** and stored server-side—no physical deed is needed.

---

### 💰 Property Taxes

- Homes are subject to **regular property taxes**.
- Taxes must be paid through the designated system or in-game method.
- If taxes go unpaid for too long, ownership will be **automatically revoked** and the home will be returned to the market.

---

### 🔁 Selling or Transferring Ownership

- Players can **sell their home** or **transfer it** to another player in-character using the proper interface or form.
- This allows for property deals, gifting, or RP-driven sales.
- Staff do not need to be involved in property transfers unless there’s a dispute or a bug.

---

### 💤 Inactivity & Repossession

- If a player is **inactive for 60 days**, their property will be **automatically reclaimed**.
- This ensures housing stays active and available to the wider community.
- Reclaimed homes may be:
  - Returned to the market
  - Auctioned or reintroduced via roleplay

> **Note:** If you’re going on an extended break and want to protect your home, open a ticket with staff beforehand.

---

### 🧠 RP Your Ownership

- Though ownership is mechanical, it’s encouraged to **roleplay home life**, maintenance, and protection.
- Your property can be:
  - A safehouse, business front, or gang hideout
  - A family ranch, a quiet cabin, or a lively townhome
  - A scene-setting anchor that helps drive your personal story

---

In BandidosRP, home is more than storage—it’s a piece of your story.  
Protect it, pay your dues, and let it evolve with your character.
