## Community Expectations

**BandidosRP** is a mature, English-speaking roleplay community. To support a professional and immersive experience, all members are expected to meet the following expectations both in and out of character:

### 🔞 Age & Maturity

- All players must be **18 years or older** to apply and participate.
- Players are expected to conduct themselves as adults. Respectful, collaborative behavior is required at all times.

### 🗨️ Communication

- **English is the primary language** for all in-character and public out-of-character communication.
- Exceptions (e.g. Spanish, French, Indigenous languages) may be granted for roleplay relevance, but must be secondary to English unless staff-approved.
- Characters who are non-verbal or speak no English must be approved by staff prior to play.

### 🎙️ Audio Requirements

- A **good-quality standalone microphone** is required.
- We do not allow any Mics that produce significant distortion or background noise.
- Players may be denied allowlist or asked to temporarily leave the server until audio quality is corrected.
- It doesn't have to be expensive, we just want to hear you.

### 🧭 Discord Requirement

- **Anyone can join and try the server without being allowlisted.** This open entry allows new players to explore and get a feel for the community before applying.
- **Allowlisted players are required to be in the [BandidosRP Discord](https://discord.gg/z7RAT5qq4S)** for access to in-game permissions, role management, and communication with staff.
- Players who wish to gain access to restricted features (e.g. weapons, advanced systems, criminal activity etc) must complete the allowlist process and join the Discord server.

### 🎭 Handling Rule Breaks

- If a rulebreak occurs during roleplay, **do not break character**.
- Finish or excuse yourself from the scene as best as possible, then report it through the Discord Support Bot.
- OOC confrontation in voice/text chat will not be tolerated.
- Players are encouraged to communicate respectfully and resolve minor issues directly before escalating to staff.

### 🧑‍⚖️ Reporting & Resolution

- Only players directly involved in a scene may submit a report.
- Reports should be used sparingly and with the intent to educate and improve—not to punish.
- Our goal is to build trust and growth. Repeated, low-effort, or toxic use of the report system may result in warnings or restriction.

### 🚫 Cheating & Exploits

- The use of **cheats, hacks, third-party tools, crosshairs, or injections** is strictly prohibited and will result in permanent removal.
- If you discover a bug or exploit, report it immediately in the [🪲bugs
  Channel](https://discord.com/channels/1343600109757136966/1372720252852633721). **DO NOT PUBLICALLY SHARE INFORMATION ABOUT THE BUG IN ANY PUBLIC FORUM (Discord, Twitch/Youtube Stream, Video etc).**

### ⚔️ Weapon Use

- All weapons are considered **lethal** and must be treated as such in roleplay.
- Sparring or mock-fighting with weapons is not allowed unless staged with staff approval.
- Suicide roleplay, self-harm roleplay, or glorification of either is **strictly forbidden**.

### ❌ Erotic & Non-Consensual Roleplay

- Erotic roleplay of any kind is not permitted.
- Forced relationships, sexual misconduct, or implications of sexual violence are not tolerated.
- Players violating this policy will be permanently removed.

### 👻 Supernatural Roleplay

- While characters may believe in myths, spirits, or curses, they may not possess **real supernatural powers**.
- Any roleplay that implies actual supernatural ability must be pre-approved by staff.

### 🌱 Promoting Positive Culture

- Always promote a **healthy, welcoming atmosphere**.
- Whether in Discord or in-game, strive to uplift others and contribute to an environment focused on **respectful, high-quality roleplay**.
